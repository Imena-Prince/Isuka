
Isuka platform is an agricultural oriented platform. It serves a center for agriculture (both crop farming and livestock farming). This platform will help to reduce the amount of production that is lost due to lack of market, also it will help farmers to easily access all necessary services to carry out their activities efficiently. It serves mainly 3 categories of users(individual farmers, distributor and cooperatives). Each User performs specific operations.


**Individual/cooperative**
Both individual farmer and cooperative perform the same functions namely:
- **Post Production** They can all post what they have harvested. To mean, if forexample they have harvested maize, there's a form which they fill that show their location,Quantity etc
- **Farming Request** They can also request to plant a specific plant in their fields. They are required to provide The UPI number so that a land Officer can verify if that field is for agriculture or if that specific crop is best suitable for that soil
- **Subsidies(not completed)** In This section, the individual will be able to ask for support like Nkunganire

  **Distributor**
- a distributor in our sytem is an individual who will see production posted by farmers and then collect it. He/she will be able to view all available production and their location so that he/she can find a market for that production and also collect that production.
 
Isuka Platform is an agricultural oriented platform as i have said before. It Serves 3 Main Core functions namely:
